# Data and Software Availability

## Manuscript-ready text

Processed molecular-pair tables, standardized structures, source identifiers, endpoint-semantics classifications, exclusion reasons, manual-review status, out-of-fold and external predictions, frozen models, environment specifications, and executable scripts are available at https://github.com/yiweiliu96-gif/F-Microsomal-Stability (version `v1.0.0`). The model-development data were obtained from Fang et al. (J. Chem. Inf. Model. 2023, DOI: 10.1021/acs.jcim.3c00160), and the external and matched-pair records were derived from ChEMBL35 through the OpenADMET microsomal-clearance catalog. These reused public records remain subject to their source licences and publication terms. No new in vitro or in vivo measurements were generated in this study.

The tagged release is the fixed snapshot associated with the submitted manuscript. A Zenodo DOI may be added in a later release after archival; no repository DOI is claimed here.
